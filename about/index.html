About

The guide I wish I'd had

I'm SJ, a British-born Barbadian. I relocated from the UK to Barbados permanently and now live in St. James.

When I made the move, the practical information I needed was scattered, out of date or buried in long forum threads. So I built the guide I wish I'd had.

I've spent years working as a data analyst, and that shapes how this site works. Facts are checked against official sources. Each section shows when it was last verified. Where a figure can't be confirmed, the guide says so rather than guessing.

This site isn't legal, immigration or financial advice. Always confirm with the official source before you act.
