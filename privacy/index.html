Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who runs this site

Moving to Barbados is run by SJ, based in St. James, Barbados.

What we collect

Your email address if you sign up for the checklist, your quiz answers, messages you send through the contact form, and resources you suggest.

Why we collect it

To send you the checklist and occasional emails about moving to Barbados, to improve the site, and to reply to your messages. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Contact messages and suggestions are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Your choices

Unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email. To see or delete the information we hold about you, send a request through the contact form.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Your checklist progress is saved in your own browser and isn't sent to us. In future this site may use analytics and advertising cookies (for example Google AdSense). This policy will be updated before that happens.

Services we use

Email signups, contact messages and suggestions are delivered through Formspree, which processes your information only to provide this service.
