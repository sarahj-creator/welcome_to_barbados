About the guide

Written by someone who made the move

I'm SJ, a British-born Barbadian now living in St. James. When I relocated, the practical information was scattered, out of date or buried in forum threads.

I've spent years as a data analyst, and that shapes how this guide works: facts are checked against official sources, every section shows when it was last verified, and where a figure can't be confirmed, the guide says so instead of guessing.
