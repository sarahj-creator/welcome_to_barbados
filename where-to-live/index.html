Where to live

Barbados has 11 parishes, and they feel very different: calm west coast, lively south coast, wild Atlantic east, green inland hills. These are general impressions to help you understand the differences, not rankings.

Not sure where to begin? Take the parish quiz, then spend time in an area at different times of day before signing a lease.
